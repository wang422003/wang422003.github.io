PhysUniBench evaluates whether multimodal models can combine visual diagrams, physical concepts, and mathematical reasoning to solve undergraduate-level physics problems. It covers eight physics subfields, from classical mechanics and electromagnetism to quantum mechanics and relativity.

{% include figure.html path="assets/img/projects/physunibench-overview.png" alt="PhysUniBench overview showing multimodal physics questions and benchmark coverage" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

The benchmark includes both open-ended and multiple-choice questions. Its visual evidence and difficulty annotations support analysis of scientific reasoning across topics and levels of complexity.

[Project website](https://prismax-team.github.io/PhysUniBenchmark/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17667)
